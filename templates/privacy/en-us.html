Who's in charge

{{ controller.name }} is hosting the parties on this site and is responsible for your data (the "data controller"). Contact: {{ controller.email }}.

What we save

  • Your name, or the name on the invite (like "Kari and Ola").
  • Your answer and how many are coming.
  • How your name shows on the guest list, and which group link you used, if any.
  • Your language, when the invite was made, and when you answered.
  • Which version of this page you saw when you answered.
  • Only if you add it: your email, if it's confirmed, and when you said yes.

We don't save your IP address. We keep it in memory for up to an hour to stop spam.

Why

  • Name, answer, and headcount: the host needs them to plan the party. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). You were invited or signed up yourself.
  • Guest list: so guests can see who else is invited. Legal basis: legitimate interest. You pick full name, first name and initial, or hidden.
  • Email: only for your link and reminders. Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)). Remove your email from your page to take it back.

Where it comes from

If you were invited, the host typed in the name. Everything else comes from you.

Who sees it

The host sees everything, and so can people the host asks to help out: some see only who's going, others everything. Other guests (anyone with a personal or group link) see your name on the guest list, and if you're going or haven't answered yet:

  • Before you answer: your first name and last initial (like "Kari N.").
  • After you say you're going: your full name, unless you picked something else, and how many you're bringing (like "+1").
  • You can switch between full name, first name and initial, and hidden anytime.
  • If you said you can't go, you're not on the list.

Other guests don't see anything else from your reply, or your email. Emails go out through MailerSend (MailerSend UAB, Lithuania), which handles your email for the host as a "processor." The site runs on the host's own server. We don't share anything with anyone else, and the site loads nothing from other websites.

How long

  • Emails not confirmed within 48 hours: deleted.
  • Record of sent emails: deleted after {{ keep_sent_mail_days }} days.
  • Everything else: deleted {{ keep_after_event_days }} days after the party.
  • You can delete it all yourself anytime.

Your rights

On your page you can see and download everything we have on you, fix your name, remove your email, and delete it all. You can also object to us using your data; then we delete your RSVP. Lost your link or have questions? Contact the host. You can complain to Norway's data protection authority, Datatilsynet.

Cookies and tracking

One cookie per invite, only if you ask for it. On your page, you can have a device remember you. Your browser then saves your personal link in a cookie, so the front page can link you back to your page. It's only set when you check the box to be remembered as you RSVP, or press the button for it after RSVPs close, only sent to this site, can't be read by scripts, and expires when your RSVP is deleted, {{ keep_after_event_days }} days after the party. RSVPing with the box unchecked removes it, and so do "Forget this device" and deleting your RSVP. The cookie can't change anything on its own: you still need your link to change your answer.

No analytics, nothing from other websites.
