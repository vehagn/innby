What we keep

We don't store your IP address. It is kept in memory for up to an hour to stop abuse.

Why

  • Name, answer and how many: the host needs them to plan the event. The legal basis is legitimate interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): you were invited, or signed up yourself.
  • The guest list: so guests can see who else is invited. The legal basis is legitimate interest. You choose between your full name, your first name and initial, and not being listed.
  • E-mail: only for your link, reminders, and word of a new time or place. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you withdraw by removing your address on your page.
  • Game scores make the host's games a friendly contest. The legal basis is legitimate interest: you take part only by playing from your page, and only your best score is kept.
  • The message board lets guests talk before the event. The legal basis is legitimate interest: you take part only by posting or voting, and you can delete your posts. Please don't write about anyone's health, beliefs or other sensitive matters there.

Where it comes from

If you were invited, the host entered the name. Everything else comes from you.

Who sees it

The host sees everything, and so may people the host asks to help: some see only who's coming, others everything. Other guests (anyone with a personal or group link) see your name on the guest list, and whether you're coming or haven't answered yet:

  • Before you answer: your first name and the initial of your last name (such as "Kari N.").
  • Once you've said you're coming: your full name, unless you chose otherwise, and how many you bring (such as "+1").
  • You can change between your full name, your first name and initial, and not being listed at any time.
  • If you've said you aren't coming, you aren't on the list.

Other guests see nothing else of your answer, and never your e-mail address. {mail_processor} {hosting} Nothing is shared with anyone else, and the site loads nothing from other websites.

If the host offers games, other guests see your best score in each, by your name as on the guest list, or as "a guest" if you aren't on it. The games run on a separate site of the host's, and get only a key that saves your scores, never your personal link.

If the host has a message board, other guests see what you post, signed with your name as on the guest list, or as "a guest" if you aren't on it; the host sees who wrote each post. Nobody sees how you voted, only each post's total. If you delete your answer, your votes go, and so does the text of your posts; a post with replies stays as "[deleted]" so the replies make sense.

Cookies and tracking

One cookie for each invitation a device remembers, and only if you ask for it. On your page, or as you sign up through a group's link, you can have a device remember you: the browser then keeps your personal link in a cookie, so the front page of this site can lead you back to your page. It is set only when you tick the box to be remembered as you answer or sign up, or press the button for it once answers have closed. It is sent to this site alone, can't be read by scripts, and expires when your answer is deleted, {keep_after_event_days} days after the event. Answering with the box unticked removes it, as do "Forget this device" and deleting your answer. The cookie gives no access by itself: changing your answer still needs your link.

No analytics, and nothing from other websites.
