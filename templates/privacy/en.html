In brief: we keep only what is needed to plan the occasion, use no tracking whatsoever, set a cookie only should you ask us to remember you, and delete everything {{ keep_after_event_days }} days after the event.

Who is responsible

{{ controller.name }} hosts the events on this site and is the data controller. Do get in touch: {{ controller.email }}.

What we keep

  • Your name, or the name on the invitation (for instance "Kari and Ola").
  • Your reply and the size of your party.
  • How your name appears on the guest list, and the group link you replied through, if any.
  • The language you used on the site, when the invitation was made and when you replied.
  • Which version of this notice you saw when you replied.
  • Only should you add it yourself: your e-mail address, whether it is confirmed, and when you gave your consent.

We do not keep your IP address. It is held briefly in memory to deter abuse of the forms, and is gone within the hour.

Why, and on what basis

  • Name, reply and size of party are needed to plan the occasion. The basis is legitimate interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): the host must know who is attending, and you were invited or replied of your own accord.
  • The guest list lets guests see who else is invited, as is customary before a party. The basis is also legitimate interest, and you decide whether you appear by full name, by first name and initial, or not at all.
  • Your e-mail address is used solely to send you your link and reminders. The basis is consent (Article 6(1)(a)). You withdraw it by removing the address from your page.

Where the data comes from

If you were invited, the host entered the name. Everything else comes from you.

Who sees it

The host sees everything, as may anyone the host asks to help organise: some see only who is attending, others everything. Other guests, that is anyone with a personal or group link, see your name on the guest list, and whether you are attending or have yet to reply:

  • Before you reply, they see your first name and the initial of your surname (for instance "Kari N.").
  • Once you have said you will attend, they see your full name, unless you have chosen otherwise, and how many you bring besides yourself (for instance "+1").
  • You may choose your full name, first name and initial, or not to appear at all, at any time.
  • Should you send your regrets, you are not on the list.

No other guest sees anything more of your reply or your e-mail address. E-mail is sent through MailerSend (MailerSend UAB, Lithuania), which handles your address on the host's behalf as a processor. The site runs on the host's own server. Nothing is shared with anyone else, and the site loads nothing from other websites.

How long

  • E-mail addresses not confirmed within 48 hours are deleted.
  • The record of sent e-mail is deleted after {{ keep_sent_mail_days }} days.
  • Everything else is deleted {{ keep_after_event_days }} days after the event.
  • You may delete everything yourself at any time.

Your rights

From your personal page you may see and download everything we keep about you (access and portability), correct your name, remove your e-mail address and delete everything. You may also object to the processing; we then delete your reply. Should you have mislaid your link or have any questions, do contact the host. You may complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

Cookies and tracking

One cookie for each invitation a device remembers, and only should you ask for it. On your page you may have a device remember you: the browser then keeps your personal link in a cookie, so that the front page of this site can lead you back to your page. It is set only when you tick the box to be remembered as you reply, or press the button for it once replies have closed, is sent to this site alone, cannot be read by scripts, and expires when your reply is deleted, {{ keep_after_event_days }} days after the event. Replying with the box unticked removes it, as do "Forget this device" and deleting your reply. The cookie grants nothing by itself: changing your reply still requires your link.

The site uses no analytics and no content from other websites.
