What we store

  • Your name, or the name on the invitation (for example "Kari and Ola").
  • Your answer and how many of you are coming.
  • The language you used on the site, when the invitation was made and when you answered.
  • Which version of this notice you saw when you answered.

We don't store your IP address. It's held briefly in memory to stop abuse of the forms, and is gone within the hour.

Why, and on what basis

  • Name, answer and number of people are needed to plan the party. The basis is legitimate interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): the host needs to know who's coming, and you were invited or signed up yourself.
  • Your e-mail address is only used to send you your link and reminders. The basis is consent (Article 6(1)(a)). You withdraw it by removing the address from your page.

Where the data comes from

If you were invited, the host entered the name. Everything else comes from you.

Who sees it

Only the host. E-mail is sent through MailerSend (MailerSend UAB, Lithuania), which handles your address on the host's behalf as a processor. The site runs on the host's own server. Nothing is shared with anyone else, and the site loads nothing from other websites.

How long

  • E-mail addresses not confirmed within 48 hours are deleted.
  • The record of sent e-mail is deleted after {{ keep_sent_mail_days }} days.
  • Everything else is deleted {{ keep_after_event_days }} days after the event.
  • You can delete everything yourself at any time.

Your rights

From your personal page you can see and download everything we store about you (access and portability), correct your name, remove your e-mail address and delete everything. You can also object to the processing; we then delete your RSVP. If you've lost your link or have questions, contact the host. You can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

Cookies and tracking

None. The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no content from other websites.
